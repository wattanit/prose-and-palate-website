<p class="dropcap">Asia's 50 Best Bars 2026 was announced on 28 July 2026 at an awards ceremony inside the Grand Theatre at Wynn Palace, Macau, with Hope & Sesame in Guangzhou taking the No. 1 spot. The extended No. 51–100 list had already been published two weeks earlier, on 14 July, giving the full ranking a proper two-stage rollout before the main event. Chinese mainland bars occupy an unusually large share of this year's list — Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Shanghai, Beijing, Chengdu, Changsha, and Hangzhou all place at least one room in the hundred.</p>

<Section heading="The Ones We've Been To" id="sec-1">

We haven't made it to Guangzhou yet. But between Bangkok, Singapore, Hong Kong, and Tokyo, we've had a drink at sixteen of the hundred bars on this year's list — three of which we've already written up in full. Here's what we made of the rest, in the order they rank.

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-leone" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/bar-leone.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Bar Leone, Hong Kong" />

**No. 3 — Bar Leone**, Hong Kong. Sharp, well-drilled classic twists, though the permanent queue and rushed-tourist energy make it feel more like a bucket-list stop than a quiet drink.
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 4 — Dry Wave Cocktail Studio**, Bangkok. Its "super classic" menu mashes pairs of classic cocktails into something new, and it's one of the best bars to appreciate minimalist drinks.

<Figure id="lb-abb-drywave" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/dry-wave-cocktail.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Dry Wave Cocktail Studio, Bangkok" />
</Row>

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-barus" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/bar-us.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Bar Us, Bangkok" />

**No. 6 — Bar Us**, Bangkok. A dining-inspired menu where the cocktails taste like the dish they're named after; imagine cuisine made liquid.
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 7 — Lennon's**, Bangkok. Thirty floors above Bangkok, where the cocktails actually earn the view instead of just decorating it. [Read our full review →](/bars/lennon)

<Figure id="lb-abb-lennons" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/lennons.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Lennon's, Bangkok" />
</Row>

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-jiggerpony" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/jigger-and-pony.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Jigger & Pony, Singapore" />

**No. 9 — Jigger & Pony**, Singapore. The best bar in Singapore looks like an ordinary hotel bar and plays it that way on purpose, backing its reputation with classics done properly rather than spectacle. [Read our full review →](/bars/jigger-and-pony)
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 12 — Nutmeg & Clove**, Singapore. A colonial shophouse bar putting a Singaporean lens on the classics, run with the efficiency of a place that knows exactly how busy it's going to be. [Read our full review →](/bars/nutmeg-and-clove)

<Figure id="lb-abb-nutmegclove" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/nutmeg-and-clove.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Nutmeg & Clove, Singapore" />
</Row>

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-sathorn" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/bar-sathorn.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Bar Sathorn, Bangkok" />

**No. 17 — Bar Sathorn**, Bangkok. Beautiful bar in a spectacular historic building, with drinks as spectacular as the venue.
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 20 — BKK Social Club**, Bangkok. A grand Four Seasons lounge leaning into Latin American flavours; the tequila drink that looks like a glass of water but tastes like a sweet, nutty snack was one of the better surprises of the year.

<Figure id="lb-abb-bkksocial" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/bkk-social-club.jpg" alt="A cocktail at BKK Social Club, Bangkok" />
</Row>

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-opium" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/opium.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Opium, Bangkok" />

**No. 25 — Opium**, Bangkok. A cozy bar in Bangkok's Chinatown, built around a Chinese-Thai-inspired drink theme.
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 26 — Virtù**, Tokyo. A French-Japanese fusion hotel lounge with spectacular drinks; the Manhattan-style Takara arrives under a flake of gold leaf, with a chilled refill kept waiting on ice.

<Figure id="lb-abb-virtu" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/virtu.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Virtù, Tokyo" />
</Row>

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-god" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/god.jpg" alt="A cocktail and food pairing at G.O.D, Bangkok" />

**No. 31 — G.O.D**, Bangkok. The whole theme runs on food pairings; on our visit that meant a spiced negroni served alongside uni on a chocolate shell.
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 53 — Bar Trench**, Tokyo. Cozy bar in Tokyo with flavourful drinks.

<Figure id="lb-abb-trench" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/bar-trench.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Bar Trench, Tokyo" />
</Row>

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-vesper" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/vesper.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Vesper, Bangkok" />

**No. 56 — Vesper**, Bangkok. Home of the famous Vesper Martini, but other drinks are equally sparkling.
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 62 — Bar Benfiddich**, Tokyo. No menu, just an omakase of drinks built to order in a small, otherworldly, herb-strewn den on the 9th floor of a Shinjuku building.

<Figure id="lb-abb-benfiddich" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/bar-benfiddich.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Bar Benfiddich, Tokyo" />
</Row>

<Row>
<Figure id="lb-abb-messenger" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/messenger-service.jpg" alt="A cocktail at Messenger Service, Bangkok" />

**No. 69 — Messenger Service**, Bangkok. A "grocery store"-inspired menu tucked on the third floor of an old building in Bangkok's Chinatown.
</Row>

<Row variant="alt">

**No. 79 — The Bellwood**, Tokyo. A small, new Shibuya bar built around playful, imaginative builds.

<Figure id="lb-abb-bellwood" src="/images/posts/bars/2026/asias-best-bars-2026/the-bellwood.jpg" alt="A cocktail at The Bellwood, Tokyo" />
</Row>
